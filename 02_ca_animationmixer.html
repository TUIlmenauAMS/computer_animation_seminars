ANIMATION CONCEPT — mixer.update(delta)
The mixer does not track wall-clock time itself — it advances by exactly the delta you pass each frame. This is why pausing is as simple as not calling mixer.update(), and scrubbing is as simple as setting action.time = value and calling mixer.update(0) to force a single evaluation at the new time without advancing the clock. Every animation tool (Blender, Unity, Unreal) works on the same principle.
ANIMATION CONCEPT — QuaternionKeyframeTrack & slerp
Rotation tracks use slerp (spherical linear interpolation) rather than plain lerp. Regular lerp of two quaternions changes speed through the arc — the angular velocity is not constant. Slerp travels the shortest arc on the quaternion unit hypersphere at constant angular velocity. This is why QuaternionKeyframeTrack always produces smooth, constant-speed rotation — even across keyframes that are far apart.
ANIMATION CONCEPT — InterpolateSmooth (CatmullRom)
Switch the position track to Smooth and watch the curve in the graph below become a flowing spline that overshoots slightly past each keyframe. THREE.InterpolateSmooth uses a CatmullRom spline — the same curve type used in Blender's and Maya's graph editors for smooth interpolation. It passes through every keyframe and computes tangents automatically from neighbouring points, producing organic motion without manually placing handles.
ANIMATION CONCEPT — The Scrubber
Dragging the Scrub slider sets action.time directly — exactly what every NLE and animation tool does when you drag the playhead. The mixer is paused during scrubbing and a zero-delta update forces it to evaluate the tracks at the new time. Notice how Discrete interpolation makes the position jump between values rather than interpolate — this is the step-function mode used for boolean properties (visibility, mesh swap) in real rigs.
